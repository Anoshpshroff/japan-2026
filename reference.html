Nine things that will bite

From cross-checking flight times, hotel dates and car plans against what's actually open in late October. Four change what you should do; the rest change when.

26 OCTThe Morioka rental desk at 20:00 is the trip's hard deadline

The booking is the last slot of that desk's day. If NH1080 slips, ring 019-622-0100 from Haneda with your train number — Toyota holds a reservation one hour past the slot, but only if you call. Past 21:00 it can be cancelled outright, leaving you in Morioka with an inn booked ninety minutes away.

26 OCTRing the inn before you fly — the booking still says 16:00–17:00

Check-in is 15:00–18:00 and you arrive about 21:30. A three-unit inn will almost certainly accommodate a late arrival it knows about, and cannot accommodate one it does not. Get the door arrangement confirmed.

2 NOVLeave Aomori at 07:00 — the 17:25 Seattle flight sets the clock

Driving back to Morioka means keeping the cars overnight on the 1st and being on the road by 07:00 — Morioka by 09:15, Narita by about 13:55. Even a snow-slowed expressway still makes Narita by ~14:05, but the margin is yours only if you start on time.

21 OCTYou land at Narita but leave from Haneda

JL933 to Miyakojima goes from HND, not NRT. Akasaka to Haneda is 35–45 minutes on the Toei Asakusa line via Sengakuji — leave the hotel by 08:15.

31 OCTOsorezan closes for the season on 31 October

If the sulphur-and-ash netherworld appeals, 30 or 31 October is the only window — shut from 1 November until May. A 2h+ drive each way, so it's a whole day, and it competes with Hirosaki.

TOHOKUSunset is around 16:35 up north

By late October Aomori loses the light shortly after 16:30, with about ten and a half hours of daylight. Gorge walks and ropeways must start in the morning — the reason the northern legs are paced as they are.

MIYAKOWater is 27 °C but cooling — a 3 mm full suit is right

Late October sits just after the typhoon season tails off — air ~28 °C, sea ~27 °C — but the sea cools from mid-month and you're doing two days of repetitive diving. 5 mm if you feel the cold.

SOLVEDThe no-fly window is no longer a constraint

Diving finishes on the 23rd and you fly at 11:50 on the 26th — nearly three days between the last tank and the cabin, far past the 18-hour minimum. The 24th and 25th are snorkel-and-beach by design, not as a buffer.

28 OCTAkita-Komagatake is open — and you can drive up

The Komagatake Line closes for winter from 1 November, so the 28th is inside the season, and the private-vehicle restriction only ran to 20 October — you drive yourselves to the 8th Station at ~1,300 m. Summit snow is the one variable.
